# 🏃‍♂️ RunHack Barcelona — Edition 2026

**RunHack Barcelona** is a hackathon where runners, developers, designers and sports-tech enthusiasts come together to build innovative solutions for the running and fitness community. Edition 2026 returns to Barcelona for a weekend of code, creativity, and kilometers.

## 📅 About

RunHack combines the energy of endurance sports with the spirit of hacking. Over one intense weekend, multidisciplinary teams design, build, and pitch projects that push the boundaries of running technology — from training apps and wearables integrations to AI-powered coaching and race-day experiences.

- **Edition:** 2026
- **City:** Barcelona, Spain
- **Format:** In-person hackathon (team-based)
- **Duration:** 48 hours

> 📍 Exact dates, venue, and registration links to be announced. Follow this repo for updates.

## 🎯 Tracks

- **Performance & Training** — tools that help runners train smarter and recover better
- **Race Experience** — apps and devices that improve the on-course experience for runners and spectators
- **Community & Social** — platforms that connect runners and build local running culture
- **Open Innovation** — anything running-related that doesn't fit the boxes above

## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Who Can Participate

RunHack Barcelona welcomes:

- Developers (frontend, backend, mobile, embedded/IoT)
- Designers (UX/UI, product)
- Data scientists & ML engineers
- Running coaches, athletes, and sports-tech enthusiasts

No prior hackathon experience required — just bring your energy and curiosity.

## 🗓️ Weekend Schedule (typical flow)

| Day | Activity |
|-----|----------|
| Friday | Kickoff, team formation, ideation |
| Saturday | Building, mentoring sessions, workshops |
| Sunday | Final sprint, demos, judging, awards |

## 🏆 Judging Criteria

- **Innovation** — originality of the idea
- **Impact** — relevance to the running community
- **Execution** — quality and completeness of the build
- **Presentation** — clarity and impact of the final pitch
